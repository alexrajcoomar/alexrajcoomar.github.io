02 Essay Independent August 2026

Crucible Cockpit

Adversarial claim auditing: decompose, attack, grade what survives

612 words0 figures2 tables10 recorded linksinstrument

A system that refuses to answer questions directly: it decomposes each one into individually falsifiable claims, files a falsifier and an evidence tier with every claim, and sets attack agents against them on provenance, inference, selection, and anachronism. The answer is whatever the surviving claims can carry, at the tier of the weakest load-bearing link. Three runs are on the record, a hindsight-locked backtest and two live audits, with graveyards, rejected attacks, and unabridged transcripts.

A pre-registered protocol on the Late Bronze Age collapse

9,237 words4 figures16 tables1 recorded links40 min

Notes on the reading

How to read a row. A slot marked section quotes a heading the piece itself carries and opens the piece at it. A slot marked linked document names another piece the corpus records a link to. The rest print a value this build already holds: the line the piece declares it was built from, or the artifact itself. A slot nothing answers says not carried, and is counted.

What is measured and what is not. The counts, the links and the figure on each entry are measured. Which heading answers which slot is a reading, declared in content/cases.json. The build checks that every anchor exists, that every heading is quoted as the invariance record holds it, and that every named document is a recorded link; it does not check that the reading is the right one. Disagree with a placement and the file is the place to say so.
