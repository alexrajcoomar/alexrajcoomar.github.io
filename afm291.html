In briefHow this vault works

Every chapter note follows one structure: frontmatter → source note → LO map → decision flowcharts → technical rules → IFRS/ASPE → worked examples → exam traps → retrieval practice → QA block.

Checkpoints and retrieval questions are collapsed by default. Click to reveal the answer only after you have attempted it. That single design choice is what turns these notes from reference documents into study tools.

Anything I added beyond the original textbook material is labelled inline — [Tutor-added], [Audit fix], [Clarification], or [CPA exam addition]. Anything unlabelled is sourced from your textbook.

Every injected standard reference carries a [VERIFY] marker. None was asserted as settled.

Cross-Cutting Themes

Concepts that recur across chapters. Written answers that connect them score better than answers that treat each chapter in isolation.

Study Sequence

  1. Chapter 1 → Chapter 2 — theory before instrument. Chapter 2 answers questions Chapter 1 raises.
  2. Chapter 3 — the machinery (accounting changes, statement structure) every later chapter assumes.
  3. Chapter 4 — first full standard application. Weeks 2–4.
  4. Chapter 6 — inventory. Pairs with the LOCM ↔ impairment logic you will meet again in Chapter 10.
  5. Chapter 8 ⚠️ — missing. Read it from the textbook before Chapter 10.
  6. Chapter 9 → Chapter 10 — Chapter 9 says which assets get tested annually; Chapter 10 supplies the mechanics.
