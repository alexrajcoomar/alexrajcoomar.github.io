5Five traps

  1. Circle exactly one option. Hedging is penalized — "ambiguous" is the answer only when channels genuinely oppose.
  2. π divides by the earlier P. Always.
  3. "Temporary" MPK shock → no I^D shift. MPK^f itself is untouched.
  4. mm is (cu+1)/(cu+res), never 1/res. 1/res only when cu=0.
  5. Numbers come out clean. Ugly arithmetic = an error upstream. Graph marks are separate — label axes, curves, both equilibria, always.
First thing when the paper opens — write your convention corner e_nom = foreign currency per 1 CAD  ·  W/P falls when P rises (wage stuck)  ·  r is the asset-market axis, not i.
