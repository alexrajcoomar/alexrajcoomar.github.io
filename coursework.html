Coursework

35 pieces across 5 courses, each one built while taking the course rather than afterwards. Built from my course materials, 11 of the 35 with AI assistance, then verified against the course materials; each piece states on its own bar what it was built from, and 8 of them declare no source on file. References are organised for retrieval under time pressure, not for reading front to back, which is why several of them are deliberately compressed to what fits on a page.
