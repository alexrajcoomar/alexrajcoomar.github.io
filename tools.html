Interactive tools

7 things you use rather than read. 3 of them install to a phone home screen. Four are drill engines that hold their question banks in code, so they carry no reading time: a drill has no length, only a session. The other two render a full document on load and are measured like one.

ToolIndependent

IFRS 15 Judgment Trainer

A drill on revenue recognition under IFRS 15: 29 determinate items scored against the standard and 33 judgment items that show their reasoning, every answer carrying its paragraph or illustrative example, verified against Part I of the CPA Canada Handbook, 2026 Edition. Each item names the trap beside the treatment, because the wrong answer you almost gave is the thing worth studying.

Installing one

How to

On a phone, open the tool and choose Add to Home Screen from the share menu. It gets an icon and opens without browser chrome. Progress is stored in that browser only: nothing is uploaded, and clearing site data clears the progress with it.
