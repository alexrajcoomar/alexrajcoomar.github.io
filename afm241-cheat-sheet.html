1 · DisruptionChristensen · the biggest block
The Innovator's Dilemma: good companies get beaten by cheaper, "worse" products because the same rational financial logic that wins their best customers blinds them to disruption from below.
Why incumbents fail (the chain): a "why did they fail?" question wants a link here — not "bad management." RPV = Resources (what you have) · Processes (how you work) · Values (what you'll fund — the part that rejects low-margin disruptors).
Classify any disruption — ask WHO it targets (not how good it is):

4 · AI Toolkitspell-outs + what each actually is
ML vs GenAI
ML (Machine Learning): learns from data to predict. Goal = predictive accuracy.
GenAI (Generative AI): LLMs on the transformer (2017); creates output. Goal = generation.
RPA vs Claude
RPA (Robotic Process Automation): rule-based, structured tasks; fixed cost; devs build.
Claude: augments cognitive work; variable cost; anyone builds.
6 Pillars of LLM security
SOC 2 (audit of vendor controls; Type 1 = design / Type 2 = over time) · permissioning · refuse prompt-injection · admin oversight · zero data retention · audit logging.
The 4Ds of AI Fluency
Delegation (what to hand AI) · Description (the goal — prompting) · Discernment (trust it? = skepticism) · Diligence (own it = you sign the file).
