What was read, and what was not

The governing text in Canada is IFRS 15 as adopted in Part I of the CPA Canada Handbook. On 21 August 2026 the whole of IFRS 15 in Part I, 2026 Edition, was read directly: the standard, Appendices A, B and C, and all sixty-five illustrative examples. The Standards and Guidance Collection was dated July 2026 at the time of reading.

Every paragraph number, example number, figure and conclusion cited in this bank was checked against that text, and no discrepancy was found. The bank was built first from the AASB and IFRS Foundation reproductions of the IASB text, then verified against the Handbook rather than assumed to match it.

Version relied on

  • Part I, 2026 Edition, the version presented as in effect on 1 January 2026.
  • Paragraph C1: effective for annual reporting periods beginning on or after 1 January 2018.
  • Paragraph C1A: IFRS 16, issued January 2016, amended paragraphs 5, 97, B66 and B70.
  • Paragraph C1B: Clarifications to IFRS 15, issued April 2016, amended paragraphs 26, 27, 29, B1, B34 to B38, B52 to B53, B58, C2, C5 and C7, deleted B57, and added B34A, B35A, B35B, B37A, B59A, B63A, B63B, C7A and C8A.
  • Appendices A, B and C only. There is no Appendix D in the Part I version.

IFRS 18 is not in the version this bank reflects. IFRS 18, issued April 2024, makes consequential amendments to IFRS 15, but the string does not appear anywhere in the Part I text in effect on 1 January 2026. Part I keeps a separate section for standards issued but not yet effective, which was not opened. Anything dated from this bank should say which version it worked from.

Two verification passes

An independent agent re-checked eleven high-risk attributions against the free sources and found one real error: paragraph 62(c) has two limbs, a reason other than the provision of finance and a difference proportional to that reason. The original wording carried only the first, which overstates how easily 62(c) is met. The Handbook pass then confirmed the corrected wording, along with the sub-clause labels in paragraph 12, the single-uncertainty reading of paragraph 54 that makes Example 21 work, and the separate treatment of the CU900 credit in Example 5 Case B.

Not covered

Warranties, repurchase agreements, bill-and-hold, consignment, customer acceptance, breakage, non-cash consideration, loyalty programmes, renewal options, transition, and ASPE Section 3400. These are absent because the bank stopped, not because the source failed. No issuer disclosures appear anywhere: nothing was invented, approximated or reconstructed to fill that gap.

One correction worth carrying

Control is not an Appendix A defined term. Appendix A defines nine terms and control is not among them. Control of an asset is described in paragraph 33, with the point-in-time indicators in paragraph 38. Checked in the Handbook itself.

Other sources

  • Four IFRS Interpretations Committee agenda decisions read in full and cited as authority: January 2019, June 2019, March 2020 and May 2022.
  • CSA Staff Notice 51-364 and 51-365, on revenue disclosure deficiencies found in Canadian continuous disclosure reviews.
  • Build-time text from the AASB 15 compiled standard and the IFRS Foundation illustrative examples.
