Study pathThe whole course, in order, from the beginning

Follow the course’s own week sequence rather than the textbook’s chapter order — AFM 291 deliberately teaches Ch. 4 (revenue) before Ch. 3 (subsequent events), and splits Chapters 7, 9 and 10 across several weeks. Note that Chapter 1 is not assigned in this course; the syllabus begins at Chapter 2. Every week follows the same rhythm: set the scope, watch the pre-lecture recordings, work the lecture deck, attempt the PBL case cold, then mark yourself against the sample solution.
