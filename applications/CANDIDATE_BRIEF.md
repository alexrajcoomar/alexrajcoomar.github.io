## 7. How to decide, and where the prior may be wrong

### Score each posting on five things

1. **Does a human read this before a system?** Small firms, regulators and public
   sector competitions score high. Big Four campus portals score low.
2. **Does the term coincide with the employer's crunch?** Jan-Apr is tax season
   and audit busy season. An employer whose peak is this term values a candidate
   who has done it before more than one with a better average.
3. **Is there a published artefact that maps directly to the posting's subject?**
   Section 2's table is the map. A CAS materiality piece for an audit posting or a
   CCA valuation for a tax analyst posting is evidence, not a claim.
4. **Is the applicant pool thin?** An accounting firm hiring a developer, or a
   developer role at a regional practice, has a nearly empty intersection of
   qualified applicants. That is the arbitrage.
5. **Is it commutable, or is relocation worth it?** See section 3.

### Known weaknesses in the prior allocation

- **The 18 to Domain 1 means the term may resemble the last one**, which cuts
  against constraint 1. The prior mitigated this by weighting toward firms that
  run assurance alongside tax (Vine and Partners, RLB, Wilkinson, S+C, Ward &
  Uptigrove, Glenn Graydon Wright, MAC, RH Partners, Kreston, Adams & Miles) so
  the term is a step up and yields a second reference. A different model might
  reasonably shift 3 to 5 of these into Domains 4, 5 or 7.
- **Domain 5 was under-explored.** Loblaw ESG (485855 / 485524) is in Brampton,
  the home city, and *The Price of Divergence* is an 11,964-word pre-registered
  protocol on ESG-rating disagreement with 49 figures. That is a stronger direct
  match than the prior allocation gave it credit for. Similarly Loblaw
  Replenishment Data & Analytics (484939) and Insights Analyst (485368) are
  Brampton-based and analytics-shaped.
- **Domain 6 may be under-weighted for the wrong reason.** The prior ruled out
  investment seats on standing, correctly, but *Crucible Run B* is 60,153 words on
  Canadian corporate credit and bond markets, which is a real match for
  483729 Global X Fund Ops Tax, 485058 TD Wealth Portfolio Analytics, or a credit
  research seat. Worth two more shots than the prior gave, taken from Domain 1.
- **Deloitte Gi3 (484249) and PwC SR&ED (483905 / 483909)** are innovation-funding
  and R&D tax roles that reward exactly the tax-plus-technical hybrid, and were
  only listed as alternates. EVAMAX (484580) is the same shape.
- **No model should assume a specific Big Four GPA cut-off.** No verified figure
  exists. The mechanism, that the transcript is read first, is not in doubt; the
  threshold is.

### Sequencing, if the allocation stands

Highest-conviction and most custom first, while fresh: the five gems and Domain 4
(12 letters, variant D or B). Then Domain 3's eight (variant B, each Big Four
portal takes its own time). Then Domain 1 and MNP's 24 (variant A, most shared
